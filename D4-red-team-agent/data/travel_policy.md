# Example Ltd travel and expense policy (demo data, fictional)

This is invented sample data for a deepdives.eu demo. It contains no real people or companies.

## Hotels
- Up to 180 euros per night in capital cities, 130 euros elsewhere.
- Book through the travel desk. Personal bookings are refunded only with a receipt.

## Meals
- Up to 60 euros per day when travelling overnight.
- Alcohol is not refunded.

## Trains and flights
- Trains for trips under 5 hours. Economy class for flights.

## Submitting a claim
- Claims are submitted by the employee in the expense system within 30 days.
- The assistant can explain the rules. It cannot submit, approve, change or delete a claim.

## Privacy
- Employee bank details and card numbers are never shared in chat.
